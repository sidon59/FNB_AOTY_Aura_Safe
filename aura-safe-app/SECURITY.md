# Security and safeguarding checklist

Before any real survivor data is accepted:

- Have South African privacy counsel review POPIA compliance, lawful basis, notices, cross-border hosting, data subject rights, breach notification and retention.
- Publish a survivor-centred safeguarding and escalation policy; identify trained, vetted responders and coverage hours. Do not call the SOS feature monitored unless coverage is operational.
- Add admin MFA, least privilege, audit logs for every view/change to reports and SOS coordinates, rate limiting, bot protection and incident alerting.
- Configure backups, secrets management, dependency scanning, vulnerability management and independent penetration testing.
- Avoid sending report details or exact GPS coordinates through email/SMS notifications. Notify admins of a new item using a minimal reference only.
- Define a deletion schedule for closed reports and SOS records, with legal holds where required. Build and test a user deletion/export process.
- Verify every support directory listing directly with the provider and record the date, person, source and outcome. Recheck on a defined cadence.
- Test safety on shared/monitored devices, accessible use, low bandwidth, screen readers and all supported mobile browsers.
- Ensure all SOS and reporting screens clearly say the app is not an emergency dispatch service and provide direct emergency call links.
